How to interpret the result

Severe / High

Review promptly; verify moisture and crop conditions in the field.

Moderate

Monitor conditions and plan irrigation based on local water availability.

Low

Continue monitoring; avoid unnecessary irrigation.

Waterlogging risk

Do not interpret this as a need for more irrigation; inspect drainage and field saturation.
